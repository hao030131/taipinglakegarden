Set in Malaysia’s historic town of Taiping, the Lake Gardens are known for their serene lakes, mature rain trees and a sense of calm that feels a world away from the everyday.

“Some places ask you to do more. This one invites you to simply be.”

Bring your camera, your favourite people, or just yourself. There’s no right way to spend a little time here.
